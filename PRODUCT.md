# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The operating team of **Maka Solutions SAS / Tecnoimportaciones** — a ~14-person
B2B/B2C retailer of professional audiovisual gear (Sony, DJI, Canon, Nikon,
Blackmagic, Nanlite, Godox, DZOFilm) across Bogotá and Medellín. They use the
system all day to run the business, not to browse it. Primary roles:

- **Juan (CEO / Admin)** — full system, oversight across every module.
- **Yuly (CFO)** — accounting, DIAN electronic invoicing, BI.
- **Sandra (Gerente Medellín)** — inventory, sales, logistics.
- **Daniela (Accounting)** — accounting, DIAN documents.
- **Juan Daniel (Creative Director)** — CRM, WhatsApp messaging.

Context of use: high-frequency operational work (quoting, order fulfilment,
multi-warehouse inventory, electronic invoicing, CRM pipelines, WhatsApp
omnichannel, international imports, warranties) under time pressure, mostly on
desktop, in Spanish, with English as a secondary language.

## Product Purpose

**Maka Omni-Commerce Ecosystem** — a single SaaS platform (ERP + CRM + WhatsApp
omnichannel) that replaces a stack of disconnected tools (Effi/Efficommerce,
Siigo, Mercatelly, Kommo). One record travels uninterrupted from first lead to
warranty resolution: every commercial action (contact → quote → order → payment
→ dispatch → delivery → warranty) updates all relevant modules in real time,
with no manual sync between systems — there is one system, not many.

Success looks like: the e-commerce and back-office operate entirely on this
platform without the legacy tools; every actor sees one real-time source of
truth; and routine work takes fewer steps than the systems it replaces.

## Brand Personality

**Trustworthy and precise.** Three words: *confiable, preciso, eficiente*. The
interface should feel like a serious instrument for running a business — calm,
exact, and in control. Tone is professional and clear, never playful or
salesy. It carries the quiet premium feel of the pro-audiovisual sector it
serves, without ever showing off. Warmth comes from clarity and responsiveness,
not from decoration.

## Anti-references

- **Playful / consumer-app aesthetics** (the explicit anti-reference): loud
  saturated colors, cartoon illustrations, mascot energy, bouncy or excessive
  animation. These erode the operational credibility the team needs.
- **Legacy ERP** (the systems being replaced — Effi, Siigo, SAP-era tools):
  dense gray data tables, ugly cramped forms, overloaded navigation. The new
  product must read as a clear upgrade, not a reskin of the old.
- Generic AI-SaaS slop is out of bounds by default (see the cross-register bans
  in the skill): cream/beige chassis, uppercase tracked eyebrows on every
  section, identical card grids, gradient hero numbers.

## Design Principles

1. **One flow, one truth.** Every action updates everything in real time; the
   UI never makes the user reconcile state across modules. Surfaces reflect the
   single source of truth (e.g. the 360° customer view, live SSE updates).
2. **Confidence through precision.** Exact data, unambiguous states, clear
   feedback. The user always knows what happened and what will happen next —
   especially around money, stock, and legally binding documents.
3. **Operator productivity first.** The shortest path to the task wins. Informed
   density, keyboard reach, fewer clicks. This is a tool for people doing the
   same job dozens of times a day, not a site for first-time visitors.
4. **Sober over flashy.** Color and motion serve meaning; they never decorate.
   Restraint is the default; emphasis is earned and rare.
5. **Right context, right moment.** Information and AI assistance appear for the
   right actor at the right step, instead of forcing module-switching to find it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.2 AA**. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥3:1; visible
focus states; full keyboard navigation; `prefers-reduced-motion` honored on
every animation. The system ships light + dark themes across six accent colors
(rose, indigo, violet, sky, emerald, amber) plus custom — every combination
must clear AA, not just the default. Fully bilingual (Spanish primary, English),
so layouts must absorb variable string lengths without breaking.
